The students' wing

Preparing students to question, organise, and serve.

The Students Islamic Organisation of India is Jamaat-e-Islami Hind's national platform for Muslim students — a campus movement built as much around study circles and Dawah as around student politics.

Founded
19 October 1982
Headquarters
Jamia Nagar, New Delhi
Reach
Active in every Indian state

Why it exists

SIO frames its own purpose around character, not just campus representation.

Dawah on campus

Presenting Islamic ideas to fellow students and encouraging practice, rather than functioning as a purely political student union.

Moral formation

Instilling what it calls virtue and moral values inside educational institutions, alongside academic life.

Interfaith dialogue

Organising iftars, discussions and joint events with students of other faiths — work recognised in a UNESCO survey of youth interfaith initiatives.

Service & relief

Taking part in social service and disaster relief efforts, often alongside Solidarity and HRS during emergencies.

How it fits together

SIO runs on the same Halqa logic as its parent body — national leadership, state zones, campus units.

National President& Secretary General

Set direction for the organisation across the country and represent it publicly.

Zonal councilsState & regional level

Elect their own office bearers and run activities suited to local campuses, as in Karnataka or Kerala.

Campus unitsWhere most members meet it

Study circles, Dawah drives and event organising happen mostly here, at the college or university level.

In print

SIO runs its own small publishing arm, in three languages.

The Companion

Its English-language magazine, aimed at students and young professionals.

Chatra Vimarsh & Rafeeq-e-Manzil

Sister publications in Hindi and Urdu, carrying the same mix of ideas and campus reporting.

A related, formally separate body — the Girls Islamic Organisation (GIO), founded in 1984 — does equivalent work for female students, also under JIH's patronage.
